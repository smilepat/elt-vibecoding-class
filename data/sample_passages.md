# 실습용 지문 샘플 (Sample Passages)

> 지문 기반 어휘 학습앱 실습용 지문 모음.
> 형식: **원문 → 빈칸 지문 → Word Bank → 타겟 어휘 + 뜻**
> (smilepat/vocabulary-db `all_passages_v5.md` 구조와 동일)
>
> 강의 모듈 1의 프롬프트에 붙여넣을 때는 **타겟 단어를 `**별표 두 개**`로 굵게** 표시해 사용하세요.

## 지문 1 — Global Teamwork (난이도: 초급 / A2~B1)

**1. 원본 지문 (Original Text)**
Many people around the world **work** together to solve big problems. They **look** for new ideas and solutions every day. When scientists **find** something new, they **tell** others about it. This helps everyone learn faster. If you have a question, you should **ask** an expert. They can help you understand difficult topics. Global teamwork is very important for our future.

**2. 빈칸 지문 (Fill-in-the-Blanks)**
Many people around the world _____ together to solve big problems. They _____ for new ideas and solutions every day. When scientists _____ something new, they _____ others about it. This helps everyone learn faster. If you have a question, you should _____ an expert.

**3. Word Bank**
`work`, `look`, `find`, `tell`, `ask`

**4. 타겟 어휘 + 뜻**
- work — 일하다, 협력하다
- look (for) — 찾다
- find — 발견하다
- tell — 말하다, 알리다
- ask — 묻다, 요청하다

## 지문 2 — Reading Habits (난이도: 중급 / B1~B2)

**1. 원본 지문 (Original Text)**
Reading every day can greatly **improve** your vocabulary. When you read, you **identify** new words in context, which helps you remember them longer. Many studies **demonstrate** that students who read often **achieve** higher scores. Reading also has a positive **impact** on writing skills. To build a strong habit, you should **maintain** a regular reading schedule.

**2. 빈칸 지문 (Fill-in-the-Blanks)**
Reading every day can greatly _____ your vocabulary. When you read, you _____ new words in context, which helps you remember them longer. Many studies _____ that students who read often _____ higher scores. Reading also has a positive _____ on writing skills. To build a strong habit, you should _____ a regular reading schedule.

**3. Word Bank**
`improve`, `identify`, `demonstrate`, `achieve`, `impact`, `maintain`

**4. 타겟 어휘 + 뜻**
- improve — 향상시키다
- identify — 식별하다, 알아보다
- demonstrate — 입증하다, 보여주다
- achieve — 성취하다
- impact — 영향
- maintain — 유지하다

## 지문 3 — Protecting Our Environment (난이도: 중상급 / B2)

**1. 원본 지문 (Original Text)**
We must **preserve** our natural environment for future generations. Human activities **generate** large amounts of waste every day. Scientists **predict** that pollution will increase unless we act now. One important **factor** is how we use energy. We should **reduce** our use of plastic and **promote** clean energy sources. Small changes by individuals can make a **significant** difference.

**2. 빈칸 지문 (Fill-in-the-Blanks)**
We must _____ our natural environment for future generations. Human activities _____ large amounts of waste every day. Scientists _____ that pollution will increase unless we act now. One important _____ is how we use energy. We should _____ our use of plastic and _____ clean energy sources. Small changes by individuals can make a _____ difference.

**3. Word Bank**
`preserve`, `generate`, `predict`, `factor`, `reduce`, `promote`, `significant`

**4. 타겟 어휘 + 뜻**
- preserve — 보존하다
- generate — 발생시키다, 만들어내다
- predict — 예측하다
- factor — 요인
- reduce — 줄이다
- promote — 촉진하다, 홍보하다
- significant — 중요한, 상당한

## 사용 안내

- **레벨 1 (강의 본 과정)**: 위 원문에서 `**굵게**` 표시된 단어가 타겟. 그대로 복사해 프롬프트에 붙여넣으면 빈칸 앱이 생성됩니다.
- **레벨 2 (AI 자동 추출)**: 굵게 표시 없이 원문만 붙여넣고 "AI가 B1 이상 핵심 어휘 5~8개를 자동 선정"하도록 요청.
- 교사 자신의 교과서/모의고사 지문도 동일한 형식으로 만들어 쓰면 됩니다.
